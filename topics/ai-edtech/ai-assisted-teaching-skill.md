# Skill: AI-Assisted Teaching Design

## Purpose

Place AI tools inside real teaching workflows as support for questioning, planning, feedback, and reflection, without replacing student learning.

## Problems This Skill Solves

- AI use stays at the demonstration level and does not become a classroom process.
- Students depend on generated answers without building understanding or evidence.
- Teachers want to improve planning efficiency but need clear boundaries.
- Online teaching materials are abundant but poorly structured.

## Workflow

1. Define the role of AI in the lesson: brainstorming, questioning, organizing, feedback, or reflection.
2. Identify the parts students must complete through observation, experiment, judgment, and explanation.
3. Provide prompt templates, citation expectations, and reflection questions.
4. Require students to keep process records and revision traces.
5. Use rubrics to separate AI-use quality from student understanding.
6. Check privacy, copyright, factual accuracy, and academic integrity risks.

## Typical Outputs

- Classroom prompt templates
- AI use guidelines
- Student reflection sheet
- Lesson planning checklist
- Online collaboration workflow
- AI-assisted feedback template

## Public Boundary

This skill does not include real student prompts, personal data, private accounts, platform promotion, paid-service descriptions, or income-related content.
