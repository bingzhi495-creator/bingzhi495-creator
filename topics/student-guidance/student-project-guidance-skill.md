# Skill: Student Project Guidance

## Purpose

Help students turn interests, questions, practice, and communication into learning projects that are explainable, evidence-based, and open to improvement.

## Problems This Skill Solves

- Students have many ideas, but the topic is too broad or scattered.
- A project lacks a plan and the process breaks down easily.
- The final product exists, but the evidence chain is unclear.
- Students cannot explain their method, contribution, or revision process during presentation.

## Workflow

1. Use an interest interview to understand what the student cares about and already knows.
2. Narrow a broad theme into a small, actionable question.
3. Create a project plan with goals, steps, materials, timeline, and risks.
4. Build process records for observations, experiments, changes, and reflection.
5. Organize the evidence chain: question, method, result, limitation, and next step.
6. Help students present and defend the project in their own words.

## Typical Outputs

- Topic interview form
- Project plan
- Process journal template
- Evidence-chain worksheet
- Presentation script structure
- Defense question list

## Public Boundary

This skill shares general guidance methods. It does not promise competition results, admissions outcomes, paid-service benefits, or any income-related return.
