# Skill: Course Development

## Purpose

Turn a broad educational theme into a course or unit that is teachable, assessable, reusable, and easy to adapt.

## Problems This Skill Solves

- A topic is too broad to become a clear lesson sequence.
- Activities exist, but the learning goals are unclear.
- A course has content, but lacks student-facing outputs.
- Teaching resources are scattered and difficult to reuse.

## Workflow

1. Identify learners, prior knowledge, course duration, and classroom constraints.
2. Break the theme into knowledge goals, skill goals, and product goals.
3. Design a learning task chain from introduction to inquiry, practice, presentation, and reflection.
4. Define observable student outputs for each stage.
5. Prepare worksheets, examples, rubrics, reflection prompts, and teacher notes.
6. Revise the course structure based on classroom evidence and learner feedback.

## Typical Outputs

- Course outline
- Unit design map
- Lesson flow
- Student worksheet
- Project brief
- Assessment rubric
- Teacher co-planning notes

## Public Boundary

This skill documents general course development methods. It does not include real school information, student identities, internal lesson plans, commercial partnerships, or income-related information.
